# 1. folders and inside pages
when we create a page in the private section of the left side in notion by clicking `+Add New`, we don't have such a thing as folder in notion, so for creating a folder for containing our files, we create a page and then click the `+` icon next to it to create inside pages and the the upper page becomes the folder and inside pages are our page and our files.

# 2. hierarchy in accessing pages
if a person has access to a upper page, it has access to lower level pages also but if they have access to lower level page, they don't have access to upper level pages.

# 3. what are blocks in notion?
In Notion, **blocks** are the individual units of content you can add to a page, such as text, images, to-do lists, or databases.

for accessing blocks, type `/` to see a list of blocks and then search for your specific block.

# 4. what are guest and team members in notion and what are their differences?
In Notion:

- **Guest**: Someone added to a **single page** (or database). They can only access what you explicitly share.
- **Team member**: Part of your **entire workspace**. They can create, view, and edit pages (depending on permissions), and appear in @mentions and collaborative features.

**Main difference**: Guests have **limited, page-level access**; team members have access to the **whole workspace** (unless restricted by admin settings).
